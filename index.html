ECMTB 2026 talk

Replicator Dynamics as a Framework for Invasion in Microbial Communities

This talk presents a replicator-dynamics framework for linking microbial invasion outcomes to resident community composition. I discuss two data contexts: co-invasion of antibiotic-resistant and wild-type E. coli strains in the mouse gut, and vaginal microbiome data where Nugent score is interpreted through an invasion-growth scale.
